Our Story

Art Of Dentistry

Every smile tells a story. Founded by Dr. M. Kumanan, MDS — Founder & Proprietor — the clinic has spent over fifteen years weaving clinical precision together with compassionate patient care.

We deliver world-class dental treatment inside a warm, patient-centric environment. Our philosophy is simple: every patient deserves a smile they are proud of, achieved through the finest techniques and materials in modern dentistry.
